# Power BI for Business Analytics: Unlocking Data-Driven Growth

Modern companies produce vast amounts of data across CRM, ERP, Google Analytics, and payment gateways. Power BI unifies these disparate sources into a strategic command center.

## Key Business Analytics Use Cases

1. **Sales & Pipeline Analytics**: Track conversion bottlenecks, rep performance, and customer win rates.
2. **Financial Margin Analysis**: Drill down into product profitability, operating expenses, and cash flow trends.
3. **Supply Chain Optimization**: Monitor inventory turnover rates, vendor delivery reliability, and stockouts.
4. **Customer Behavior Insights**: Analyze cohort retention, lifetime value (LTV), and churn probability.
