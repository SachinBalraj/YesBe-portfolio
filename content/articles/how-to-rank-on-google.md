# How to Rank on Google: Proven Strategies for Modern Search

Ranking on page 1 of Google is no longer about keyword stuffing. It's about delivering the most authoritative, comprehensive, and fast-loading answer to the searcher's query.

## Proven 4-Step Google Ranking Framework

1. **Target Commercial & Informational Intent**: Build articles that directly answer buyer questions.
2. **Create Content Topic Clusters**: Group related articles around core pillars (e.g. ERP, AI, Power BI) linked together.
3. **Optimize User Experience (UX)**: Fast load times, clean typography, zero intrusive popups.
4. **Earn High-Quality Backlinks**: Secure editorial links from respected industry publications.
