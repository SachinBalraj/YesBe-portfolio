# What is CRM? A Complete Guide to Managing Customer Relationships

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is the master engine behind predictable B2B and B2C sales growth. It organizes lead contact details, email chains, phone logs, deal values, and status stages in one accessible place.

## Core Features of a Modern CRM

- **Contact & Account Management**: Complete history of every interaction with prospective and existing clients.
- **Visual Sales Pipeline**: Kanban board of deal stages from Initial Contact to Contract Signed.
- **Automated Task Reminders**: Never miss a scheduled follow-up call or proposal submission deadline.
- **Reporting & Forecasting**: Accurately predict upcoming quarterly revenue based on deal probability.

## ERP vs CRM Synergy

While CRM manages front-office lead acquisition and customer communication, ERP handles back-office order fulfillment, billing, and inventory. Connecting both delivers seamless end-to-end efficiency.
