# AI Chatbots Explained: From Rule-Based Bots to Smart Agents

Customer expectation for instant replies has never been higher. Modern AI chatbots go far beyond old-school decision trees, engaging in natural, context-aware conversations.

## How Modern AI Chatbots Work

1. **Large Language Model (LLM)**: Understands intent, nuance, and sentiment across languages.
2. **Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)**: Connects the LLM directly to your product documentation, FAQs, and database.
3. **Action Execution**: Integrates with APIs to update order status, issue refund requests, or schedule consultations.

## Why Businesses Choose Custom AI Chatbots

- **Zero Hallucination Control**: Ground responses in verified company documents.
- **Multilingual Support**: Communicate with global customers instantly in over 50 languages.
- **Seamless Human Handoff**: Complex issues get routed automatically to human support agents with full conversation context.
